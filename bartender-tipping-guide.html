How Much to Tip a Bartender: $1 a Drink or 20%?

Both rules are right — they just apply to different orders. Simple drinks get dollars per drink; complicated orders get a percentage. Here is how bartenders actually want you to think about it.

Quick answer Tip $1–2 per simple drink (beer, wine, basic cocktail) or 20% of the tab for complex cocktails and large rounds. At an open bar, $1–2 per drink in cash keeps you remembered.

$1–2 per drink vs 20%: when each applies

The per-drink rule exists because pouring a beer takes ten seconds — 20% of a $5 beer ($1) lands in the same place anyway. The rules diverge on cocktails: 20% of a $14 old fashioned is $2.80, which correctly reflects three minutes of skilled work. Use dollars for simple, percentage for complex.

Open bars

The drinks are free; the labor is not. Tip $1–2 per drink in cash — bartenders at weddings and corporate events work brutally hard for an audience that often tips nothing. Bring small bills. This is also the single cheapest way to get remembered: the bartender will prioritize the person tipping cash over the crowd waving empty glasses.

Complex cocktails earn the percentage

A smoked, fat-washed, egg-white cocktail represents years of practice. If your drink took longer to build than to drink, 20%+ is the respectful number. The same principle behind tipping more for real effort applies at the bar rail.

Tipping on tabs

Running a tab? Tip 20% on the pre-tax total when you close out — same as restaurant tipping. Do not tip per round and on the tab; pick one. And if the bar adds an auto-gratuity for large parties (common at clubs), read the receipt before adding more.

Become a regular they remember

Bartenders have long memories for two kinds of customers: great tippers and terrible ones. Tipping $2 on the first drink of the night — before you are "supposed" to — marks you as the former, and your second drink will arrive faster. It is the best $2 in nightlife.

Bar food delivery: if the bar sends food out through a delivery app, the driver is a separate worker — the bar tip does not cover them. A a delivery tip calculator keeps the two tips straight.

Waiting for a table while you drink? Coffee-shop counter etiquette is the daytime version of the same per-drink logic.

Frequently asked questions

Do I tip on happy-hour prices or full prices?

Full prices. The $4 happy-hour beer took the same pour as the $7 one. Tip like the discount never happened.

What if the bartender was rude?

Drop to $1/drink or 10% and take your business elsewhere next time. One bad night is not worth a scene; a pattern is worth a new bar.

Do I tip for water or soda?

$1 if they served it with care at a busy bar. Nothing if you grabbed it from a self-serve station.

How much for a complicated round of shots?

$1 per shot is the floor; 20% of the round total if they are craft cocktails. When in doubt, round up — shots are fast money for bartenders and they notice.
